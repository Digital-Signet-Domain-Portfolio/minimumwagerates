Rate of record

UK minimum wage rates data download

Every UK statutory NMW/NLW figure since 1999, available as CSV or JSON. Open Government Licence v3.

Files

  • nmw-rates.csv - comma-separated, header row.
  • nmw-rates.json - same content, JSON array.

Columns

  • effective_from - ISO date
  • label - human label
  • nlw - NLW (or 0 pre-2016)
  • age_18_to_20 - 18-20 band (or development rate pre-2010)
  • age_16_to_17 - 16-17 band (0 pre-2004)
  • apprentice - apprentice rate (0 pre-2010)
  • accommodation_offset_daily - £/day cap

Licence

Source data is statutory UK government publication, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3. Use freely for research, commercial work or republication. Attribution appreciated.

Refresh

The dataset is regenerated each time a new statutory rate is published (annually in autumn for the following April). The most recent regeneration was on the verification date stamped at the top of every page.
