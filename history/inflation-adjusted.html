What the trajectory shows

  • 2016 NLW launch (£7.20) is worth roughly £10.90 in 2026 pounds - the cash floor has materially outrun CPI.
  • The 2020 NLW (£8.72) translates to about £11.95 today - close to the 2025/26 cash floor.
  • The 2022 NLW (£9.50) lagged CPI inside the year as inflation peaked; the 2023 9.7% uplift was a catch-up.
  • Today's £12.71 NLW is the highest in real terms since the NMW began.
