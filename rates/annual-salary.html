Minimum wage annual salary: £24,784.50 a year in 2026/27

A full-time worker aged 21 or over on 37.5 hours a week earns at least £24,784.50 a year before tax, or £2,065.38 a month, at the £12.71 National Living Wage in force from 1 April 2026. The hourly rate is gov.uk's; the yearly figure is that rate x 37.5 hours x 52 weeks.

How we worked these figures out

  • Hourly rates: gov.uk National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates page, 2026/27 and 2027/28gov.uk.
  • Per week = hourly rate x weekly hours. Per year = per week x 52. Per month = per year / 12.
  • All figures are gross (before income tax, National Insurance, pension or student loan). Holiday weeks are counted as paid at the normal rate.
  • The law sets an hourly floor, not a salary. If your hours vary, your pay must still average at least your band's rate over each pay reference period.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage annual salary in the UK in 2026?

For a worker aged 21 or over on 37.5 hours a week, the National Living Wage of £12.71 an hour comes to £24,784.50 a year before tax (£12.71 x 37.5 hours x 52 weeks). On 40 hours a week it is £26,436.80. The rate applies from 1 April 2026.

How much is minimum wage per month in the UK?

At £12.71 an hour and 37.5 hours a week, gross pay is £2,065.38 a month (the annual figure divided by 12). On 40 hours it is £2,203.07 a month.

What is the minimum annual salary for an 18 to 20 year old?

At the 18 to 20 rate of £10.85 an hour, 37.5 hours a week comes to £21,157.50 a year. For 16 and 17 year olds and first-year apprentices, £8.00 an hour comes to £15,600.00.

What will the minimum wage salary be from April 2027?

The National Living Wage rises to £13.21 an hour on 1 April 2027. On 37.5 hours a week that is £25,759.50 a year, £975 more than in 2026/27.

Is there a legal minimum annual salary in the UK?

No. The UK minimum wage is an hourly rate. A salaried worker's pay must average at least the hourly rate for their band across each pay reference period, so the minimum salary depends on the hours worked.
